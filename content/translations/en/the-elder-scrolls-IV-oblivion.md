# 🎮 The Elder Scrolls IV: Oblivion

Developed by Bethesda Game Studios and released in 2006, **The Elder Scrolls IV: Oblivion** is a landmark of the RPG genre. It followed Morrowind and preceded Skyrim.

## 🧙‍♂️ General information
- **Full title:** The Elder Scrolls IV: Oblivion
- **Release date:** 20 March 2006
- **Developer:** Bethesda Game Studios
- **Publisher:** Bethesda Softworks & 2K Games
- **Platforms:** PC, Xbox 360, PlayStation 3

## 🌍 Story and world

Oblivion takes place in Cyrodiil, in the heart of Tamriel. Following the assassination of Emperor Uriel Septim, Oblivion Gates open and Daedric creatures invade the world.

**Objective:** Find the missing heir and save the world from the Daedric invasion.

> The central themes are the struggle between chaos and order, prophecy and the weight of fate.

## ⚔️ Gameplay
- **Open world:** A vast explorable map.
- **Character progression:** Customisation through ancestry, classes and skills.
- **Perspective:** First- or third-person view.
- **Mod support:** Community contributions have kept the game alive for years.

## 🏰 Highlights
- **Radiant AI:** NPCs follow daily routines.
- **Oblivion Gates:** Hell-like dungeons leading to other dimensions.
- **Factions:** Thieves Guild, Dark Brotherhood, Fighters Guild and Mages Guild.

Each faction has its own quest chain and story.

## 🧩 DLC and expansions
- **Shivering Isles:** A mind-bending realm ruled by Sheogorath, the god of madness.
- **Knights of the Nine:** A story of divine quests and sacred relics.

## 🏆 Why did it become legendary?
- Groundbreaking graphics for its time.
- Deep storytelling and quality dialogue.
- Freedom to be a mage, assassin or traveller.
- Still playable and relevant thanks to mods.
