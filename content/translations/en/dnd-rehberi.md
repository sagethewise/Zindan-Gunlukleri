# World Fantasia 👋

## What is FRP? What does D&D mean?

**FRP (Fantasy Role Playing):** A story-driven game genre based on imagination, played at a table or digitally, in which players portray characters.

**D&D (Dungeons & Dragons):** One of the best-known and most widely played FRP systems. It features medieval fantasy worlds filled with dungeons, dragons, magic and adventures.

---

## 🎲 What to know before playing

### A. You need a game master (DM/GM)

The game master runs the game, tells the story, brings the world to life and controls non-player characters (NPCs). If you are playing for the first time, starting with an experienced DM is ideal.

### B. Create a character

Each player creates a character, choosing gender, ancestry, class and attributes. For D&D 5e, you can create a character on [D&D Beyond](https://www.dndbeyond.com/).

### C. Roll the dice

The game uses the d20 system: a twenty-sided die is the main die. Other dice include d4, d6, d8, d10, d12 and d100.

---

## 👤 Creating your first character

### A. Choose an ancestry

- **Human:** Balanced and adaptable
- **Elf:** Quick, graceful and magically inclined
- **Dwarf:** Resilient and strong in combat
- **Tiefling / Dragonborn:** More unusual choices

### B. Choose a class

- **Fighter:** A master of combat
- **Wizard:** Uses powerful spells
- **Rogue:** An expert in stealth
- **Cleric:** Has healing spells

### C. Character attributes (six core stats)

- Strength
- Dexterity
- Constitution
- Intelligence
- Wisdom
- Charisma

### D. Personality and background

- What is their past?
- What motivates them?
- Example: “Their family was killed by a dragon, and they seek revenge.”

---

## 🧩 The basic structure of play

### A. The DM narrates

> “You are in a village whose people are threatened by a monster.”

### B. Players decide what to do

> “I want to talk to the villagers.”  
> “Let's go into the forest and find the creature.”

### C. Roll the dice

> “Roll a d20 and add your Dexterity modifier to open the lock.”

---

## 💡 Tips for players

- Think like your character.
- Cooperate with your party.
- Focus on having fun rather than the rules.
- Respect your DM's world.
