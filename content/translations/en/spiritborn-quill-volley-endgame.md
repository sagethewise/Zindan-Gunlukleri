## 🧩 Paragon boards
The original aims to increase critical chance, projectile damage, Dexterity scaling and attack speed. Suggested order:
1. Spiritborn Start Board
2. Deadshot (critical focus)
3. Sniper's Path (ranged mastery)
4. Exploit Weakness (vulnerable + critical)
5. Precision Tree (optional/hardcore)

## 🎮 Gameplay and rotation
Mobility and burst damage require positioning, timing and energy management.

1. Spirit Dart — generate energy.
2. Windstep — reposition.
3. Quill Volley — repeated AoE.
4. Spirit Surge — burst during buff.
5. Dodge + Quill Volley — trigger critical effects.

Use as many volleys as possible during Spirit Surge.

### Elite packs
Enter with Windstep, burst with Quill Volley and use crowd control for Control glyph synergy.

### Bosses
Keep moving. Dodge every two or three volleys, use Spirit Overflow for sustained energy, and Windstep to avoid boss mechanics while combining escape and damage.

### Crowd control
Slow, Chill and Immobilize control enemies. The original attributes 50%+ damage to Exploit Weakness against controlled enemies. Quill Volley pierces multiple targets, allowing chained control.

### Farming tips
High mobility suits Helltide chest routes. Spirit Mastery increases energy gain for sustained attacks. Avoid wasting abilities between enemies and use short rotations to preserve resources.

## 🚀 Content performance
- Helltide: S tier — high clearing power
- Nightmare Dungeons: A tier
- Bosses: B tier — weaker single-target damage

> The original recommends combining Razorfeather Storm and Control Glyph for elite packs.
