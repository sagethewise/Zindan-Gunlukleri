# 🎮 Diablo IV: 2023–2025 review and seasonal updates

## General information
| Feature | Details |
|---|---|
| Release date | 5 June 2023 (Deluxe & Ultimate: 2 June) |
| Platforms | PC, PS4, PS5, Xbox One, Xbox Series X/S |
| Developer / publisher | Blizzard Entertainment |
| Genre | Action RPG (hack and slash) |

## 🧩 Gameplay and innovations
- Open-world Sanctuary.
- Mounts for travelling on horseback.
- PvP in Fields of Hatred.
- Extensive skill tree and Paragon customisation.
- Live-service approach with regularly updated seasons.

|---|---|

## 📅 Seasonal updates
### Season 1: Season of the Malignant (July 2023)
Malignant Hearts and new powers; the requirement to create a seasonal character was controversial.

### Season 2: Season of Blood (October 2023)
Vampiric powers and boss revisions were generally positively received.

### Season 3: Season of the Construct (January 2024)
A construct companion and trap-filled dungeons; content variety was criticised.

### Season 4: Loot Reborn (May 2024)
A major loot and item overhaul, Tempering and Masterworking. This was one of the most anticipated seasons.

## 📅 2025 seasons
### Season 7: Season of Witchcraft (January–April)
New witchcraft powers, a raven pet and the Armory for saving builds.

### Season 8: Belial's Return (April–July)
Belial, Lord of Lies, as the main boss, Apparition Incursions and the Reliquary system for a personalised Battle Pass.

### Season 9: Sins of the Horadrim (July–September)
Horadric powers, dungeon updates and keyboard/mouse support on consoles.

### Season 10: Infernal Chaos (September–December)
Infernal Horde updates and a second IP crossover, described as unannounced in the original article.

## 🔮 Future plans (2026 onwards)
The original article listed Vessel of Hatred (late 2024), possible new classes such as Paladin or Amazon, global leaderboards and IP collaborations in Seasons 8 and 10. These are historical notes and expectations, not current announcements.

## 🧠 Overall assessment
Diablo IV combines its classic dark atmosphere with modern RPG systems. The live-service structure may feel repetitive to some players. From the perspective of this 2025 article, seasons were becoming deeper and more varied, with systemic improvements.
