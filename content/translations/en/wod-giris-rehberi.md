## 🧛 What is World of Darkness?

World of Darkness (WoD) is a series of tabletop role-playing games set in a dark version of the modern world. Its most popular system is *Vampire: The Masquerade*.

### 💡 Key concepts

- **Clans:** Vampire groups such as Brujah, Toreador and Nosferatu.
- **Masquerade:** The rule requiring vampires to conceal their identities.
- **Humanity:** A score representing your human values.

### 🎲 How do you play?

Players portray supernatural characters living in the modern world. The game progresses through storytelling and dice mechanics.

## 🎭 Why should you play?

- Rich role-playing opportunities
- Ethical conflicts and dark themes
- A gothic-punk atmosphere
