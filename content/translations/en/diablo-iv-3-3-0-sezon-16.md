## When does Season 16 begin?

Season of Tyrael’s Gambit is scheduled for 1 December 2026 at 9:30 a.m. PT. Patch 3.3.0 (build 74415) should not yet be treated as live. Builds prepared for the public test realm will need a new check against the final changes.

## Gear: keep an eye on Horadric items

The notes describe a higher Horadric item drop rate, a new seasonal transformation recipe, and separate Horadric affix categories for armour and weapons. When planning a build, the relevant affix and its relationship with your Talisman set deserve as much attention as the item name.

Rather than treating a pre-season gear list as final, we recommend checking how an item's effects support your chosen skills before prioritising upgrades.

## War Plans and Helltides

Blizzard is introducing two War Plans progression trees and adjusting Helltide schedules to reduce interruptions. These changes are particularly interesting for players who prefer a comfortable, deliberate endgame route over racing through every activity.

## Our take

For the first days of the season, consider gear accessibility, survivability and how enjoyable a build feels alongside its damage potential. In Build Forge, we will keep Season 15 and 16 data separate and update equipment and affix records once the live version is verified.
