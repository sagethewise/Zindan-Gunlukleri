> Historical PTR coverage published 11 November 2025. The following describes the test build and its announced values at the time, not current live-game mechanics.

The **Diablo IV 2.5.0 PTR** arrived ahead of Season 11. New loot, armour and resistance systems, improved monster AI and the return of the Lesser Evils reshape Sanctuary.

## ⚙️ Tempering and Masterworking
Blizzard redesigned the item journey across difficulty tiers in response to community feedback.

### Tempering
Players directly select the affix from Tempering Recipes instead of rolling randomly. Each item can have one tempered affix. Tempering Charges can be restored indefinitely, with no attempt limit.

### Masterworking
Masterworking increases item Quality rather than individual affix values, directly improving base damage, armour or resistance.
- Maximum Quality: 20.
- Each step adds a random +2 to +5 Quality.
- At maximum Quality, a final Capstone upgrade converts a random normal affix into a Greater Affix.
- The Greater Affix can be rerolled without resetting Quality, using Obducite and Neathiron.

Non-unique items now have four base affixes instead of three. The goal is less randomness, more control and more build variety.

## ✨ Ascend to legendary power with Sanctification
The High Heavens recognise Sanctuary's defenders. Sanctification is the final step of the item journey, imbuing gear with angelic power.

![Sanctification](/images/heavenly-forge.png)

### Effects
Sanctifying an item may grant one or more of these outcomes:
- A bonus Legendary Power.
- Upgrade an existing affix to a Greater Affix.
- Add an affix from the Sanctification pool.
- Replace an existing affix with a random Sanctification affix.
- Make the item indestructible, preventing durability loss.

**Warning:** Sanctified gear can no longer be modified or upgraded.

### How to sanctify
1. **Pre-Torment:** Defeat a Lesser Evil, for example during Helltide, to gain a chance to sanctify one item at the Heavenly Anvil. If you do not use the invitation immediately, wait for another.
2. **Torment:** Earn Heavenly Sigils, available only in Torment, and use them to teleport to the Heavenly Forge to sanctify Ancestral items.

Once sanctified, an item has reached its final form.

## 👹 Monster combat evolves
Sanctuary's creatures evolve to overcome their prey.

### Improved behaviour
Every monster type now has a distinct combat identity. Reactions are less predictable, more dynamic and strategic, changing with player position, attack style and defence.

### Deadlier enemies
- Packs test defences more frequently, surrounding, retreating and attacking again.
- Monster affixes are more effective.
- Groups are more spread out, making one-shot clearing harder.
- Champion rarity was adjusted; champions appear in packs.

### Elite updates
More than 20 new monster affixes were added. Elites spawn with minions inheriting some of their affixes. Designs reduce repetition and improve visual clarity throughout Sanctuary.

## 🛡️ Defence, life and Renown
The PTR redesigns defence, potions and Renown to improve class balance and strategic survivability.

### Toughness
Damage reduction is redesigned around effective health across classes:
- Armour and Resistance are ratings with diminishing returns at high values.
- Armour reduces all damage types, not just physical damage.
- Physical Damage Resistance is added.
- Torment armour and resistance penalties are removed.
- Skills and Paragon nodes that previously reduced damage are adjusted to provide multiplicative bonuses to armour or all resistances.

Toughness represents raw damage survivability, factoring in damage reduction sources. It appears below Attack Power on the character sheet. Hovering shows effectiveness against individual damage types.

### Potions and healing
- Base potion capacity: four, irrespective of previous Renown.
- Each potion instantly restores 35% of maximum life.
- One potion replenishes automatically every 30 seconds, in addition to pickups.
- Life on Hit returns and is improved.
- Life regeneration, Life on Kill and percentage healing are increased and more accessible.
- Potion upgrades are removed because healing scales with maximum life.

### Fortify
Fortify becomes a healing reservoir rather than damage reduction.
- Accumulates up to maximum life.
- When below maximum life, the character heals each second for a percentage of maximum life, consuming an equal amount of Fortify.
- Any amount of Fortify counts as Fortified.
- The mechanic works with the new healing and potion systems.

### Renown
Seasonal and Eternal realms receive changes to integrate Renown with character and build progression; seasonal specifics are explained below.

## 😈 The Lesser Evils return
The Lesser Evils invade seasonal hell-themed activities.

![Lesser Evils](/images/asmodan.png)

| Evil | Activity | Effects |
|---|---|---|
| Duriel | Helltide | Parasitic invasions and maggots |
| Belial | The Pit | Illusions, mind games and clones |
| Andariel | Kurast Undercity | Cursed spirits and time-draining attacks |
| Azmodan | World Boss | A threat throughout Sanctuary |

### Azmodan
Azmodan appears as a World Boss in multiple regions. Summoning Altars south of Zarbinzet let players summon him at will.
- Three altars summon versions influenced by the other Lesser Evils.
- Supporting monsters inherit affixes of that Evil.
- Defeat surrounding enemies before summoning.
- Chests appear after his defeat and can be opened using Corrupted Essence.

### Duriel
Duriel replaces the Blood Maiden in Helltide.
- At maximum Threat, Pangs of Duriel replace Hellborne.
- Kill them for Baneful Hearts to summon Duriel.
- Helltide packs include Maggots and enormous Portent of Pain mega-maggots.
- Hellwyrm ejects maggots instead of magma; falling meteors are replaced by maggot swarms.

### Belial
- Belial Eyes appear at random in The Pit. Destroying them stuns nearby enemies and spawns Belial Apparitions.
- Killing apparitions increases Pit progress.
- Destroy several Eyes in a run and Belial becomes the final boss; defeating him grants an additional Glyph upgrade and rewards.
- Belial creates enemy clones throughout the activity.

### Andariel
- Corrupted Tormented Spirit Beacons in Kurast Undercity are more dangerous but grant more attunement.
- Three new Andariel-specific Tributes increase rewards, guarantee Andariel as the final boss and add dungeon affixes.
- Shades of Andariel hide in dark areas and steal time with attacks.
- Wailing Spirits possess enemies and launch attacks targeting your location.
- Defeating Andariel grants exceptional rewards.

### Divine Favor and season progression
Purified or Corrupted Gifts from Lesser Evil servants grant Divine Favor and advance the Seasonal Reputation Board.

## 🌟 Earn Divine Gifts
Angel Hadriel returns with powers from the High Heavens. Gifts offer rewards and opportunities against the Lesser Evils.

![Divine Gifts](/images/divine-gifts.png)

Each Divine Gift has a Reward, Corrupted Gift and Purified Gift. Equip Purified effects on the inner ring and Corrupted on the outer ring.
- Equipped gifts activate their reward bonus.
- Corrupted gifts add difficulty while preserving rewards.
- Purified gifts remove the negative effect, add a positive one and double the reward.
- Corrupted slots are open initially; Purified slots unlock via reputation.

| Divine Favor rank | Lesser Evil unlocked |
|---|---|
| II | Duriel |
| IV | Andariel |
| VI | Azmodan |
| VIII | Belial |

On the first defeat of a Lesser Evil, take its Corrupted Essence to Hadriel to unlock two related gifts. Killing that Evil or its minions levels the gifts.

- Earned by defeating Andariel in Undercity.

Every Gift carries a risk; every purified version offers a reward.

## 🏰 The Tower and Leaderboards (beta)
The Tower is a timed, multi-floor dungeon for competitive progression. Complete the highest tier possible. The initial beta was designed alongside monster AI changes and intended to evolve with feedback over several seasons.

### Access
Unlock it through Cerrigar's Obelisk. Start runs at difficulties equivalent to unlocked Pit tiers. After leaving the starting circle, you have ten minutes.

### How runs work
1. Progress through floors with random tilesets and monster families.
2. Kill monsters and collect Orbs; higher-XP monsters award more points.
3. Destroy Tower structures to reveal Pylons. Four are guaranteed: the first three appear in random order, each once; the fourth repeats one randomly. Use temporary buffs strategically.
4. Elites, Champions and Goblins award additional Orbs.
5. Once enough progress is made, a random Tower Boss appears. Defeat it before time expires to finish and enter the leaderboard.

### Leaderboard beta
The historical PTR leaderboard ran 21–28 October (PDT). Completed Tower tier and time are displayed; access rankings at Cerrigar's Artificer's Obelisk or Collections. The PTR focused on system testing; detailed rewards and ranking schedules were expected after Season 11 launch.

### Participation and categories
Cross-platform play is required for full leaderboard access; Hardcore and Normal are separate. Categories: solo Barbarian, Necromancer, Sorcerer, Rogue, Druid, Spiritborn, plus groups of two, three and four. Rankings show rank, name, tier, time and date. Filters include all platforms/PC only, Hardcore/Normal, friends and clan members.

## 🏅 Season Rank
Season Journey returns as Season Rank. Each new rank requires a Capstone Dungeon; players may attempt it before the recommended level or difficulty.

### Renown changes
Renown is replaced by Season Rank in the seasonal realm but remains permanent in Eternal. Complete tasks to earn skill points, Smoldering Ashes and cosmetics.
- Ranks 1–3: extra Skill Points.
- Ranks 3–6: extra Paragon Points.
- Extra potion capacity is removed from Eternal Renown rewards and replaced with another bonus.
- Altars of Lilith and Tenets of Akarat provide XP and small temporary rewards rather than permanent stats.

Each rank links to a themed Capstone Dungeon with its own boss and power test. Divine Gifts, cosmetics and permanent titles reward progression.

These fixed-difficulty challenges test progress between difficulty tiers; they do not scale to character level.

## 🧪 PTR features and test content
Test regions: North America, Europe, Korea and Japan, South America and Australia. Mrak, the PTR boost NPC near major city waypoints, offered segmented boosts so players could test specific systems or items.

### Crafting and materials
Grant Gold, Grant Obols, Grant All Materials, Unlock Masterworking, Grant All Temper Manuals, Grant Lair Keys, Grant Tributes, Grant Infernal Compasses, Grant Nightmare Escalation Sigils.

### Equipment
Grant All Runes, Max All Aspects, Grant Random Legendaries, Grant All Uniques.

### Paragon
Grant All Glyphs, Max All Glyphs.

### Progression
Complete Campaign & All Strongholds (unlocks Whispers); Set Level to 50/60; Unlock All Waypoints & Reveal Map; Unlock All Torment Tiers; Set Paragon to Level 200.

### Seasonal
Grant Divine Gifts, Maximize Divine Gift Ranks, Grant Seasonal Progression Items, Grant Sanctification Stones. These were new or updated options versus the prior PTR.

## ⚙️ How to install the PTR
![PTR installation](/images/ptr.png)

The historical installation steps were:
1. Open Blizzard Battle.net and select Diablo IV.
2. Open Game Version above Play.
3. Select Public Test Realm (PTR).
4. Install the PTR client, then select Play.
5. Choose a test server and create a test character. Choose Seasonal Character to test seasonal content.
6. Enter the game and explore the 2.5.0 PTR.

PC Game Pass and Game Pass Ultimate users could access Battle.net via the Xbox app and follow the same steps. This specific test period is historical.
