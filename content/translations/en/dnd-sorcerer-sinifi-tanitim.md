## ❤️ Hit points
Hit die: 1d6 per Sorcerer level. Starting HP: 6 + Constitution modifier. Each later level: 1d6 (or 4) + Constitution modifier.

## 🛡️ Proficiencies
- Armour and tools: none.
- Weapons: daggers, darts, slings, quarterstaffs, light crossbows.
- Saving throws: Constitution and Charisma.
- Choose two skills from Arcana, Deception, Insight, Intimidation, Persuasion and Religion.

## 🎒 Equipment
A light crossbow with 20 bolts or any simple weapon; a component pouch or arcane focus; a dungeoneer's pack or explorer's pack; two daggers.

## 🪄 Class features
### Level 1
Spellcasting uses Charisma and innate power. You begin knowing four cantrips. Spells consume slots and are known rather than prepared. Choose a Sorcerous Origin (e.g. Draconic Bloodline or Wild Magic) that provides features at later levels.
### Level 2
Font of Magic grants Sorcery Points. Flexible Casting lets you spend points to create slots or sacrifice slots for points.
### Level 3
Metamagic lets you customise spells, for example targeting two creatures using Twinned Spell. Choose two options initially.
### Levels 4, 8, 12, 16, 19
Ability Score Improvement or a feat.
### Levels 5, 7, 9, 11, 13, 15, 17
Higher-level spells become available, beginning with third-level spells at level 5.
### Level 6
Gain a feature from your Sorcerous Origin.
### Level 10
The source lists an additional origin feature and another Metamagic option.
### Level 14
Another powerful active or passive origin feature.
### Level 18
The source describes Sorcerous Restoration as regaining four Sorcery Points after rest.
### Level 20
The source calls the capstone Sorcerous Mastery and describes unlimited slot creation and repeated ninth-level spells. This claim is not verified and should not be used as a rules reference.

## 🧬 Sorcerous origins
### 🐉 Draconic Bloodline
- **Level 1 — Dragon Ancestor:** Choose a dragon type such as red/fire or blue/lightning. It affects elemental spell bonuses and resistance at level 6.
- **Level 1 — Draconic Resilience:** Innate armour and additional health: +1 maximum HP at first level and another per level; unarmoured AC = 13 + Dexterity modifier.
- **Level 6 — Elemental Affinity:** Add Charisma modifier damage to spells matching the dragon's element. The source describes spending one Sorcery Point for one round of resistance; verify duration with your rules.
- **Level 14 — Dragon Wings:** Grow wings with flying speed equal to walking speed; armour may interfere.
- **Level 18 — Draconic Presence:** Spend five Sorcery Points to create an aura that frightens or charms nearby enemies, with concentration; the source lists one minute.

### 🎲 Wild Magic
- **Level 1 — Wild Magic Surge:** The DM may ask for a d20 roll when you cast a spell. A roll of 1 triggers an effect from the Wild Magic Surge table, ranging from a fireball to strange flower effects.
- **Level 1 — Tides of Chaos:** Gain advantage once on an attack, ability check or saving throw. The DM may trigger a Wild Magic Surge after use.
- **Level 6 — Bend Luck:** Spend a reaction and two Sorcery Points to roll a d4 and add or subtract it from another creature's attack, check or save.
- **Level 14 — Controlled Chaos:** Roll twice on the Wild Magic Surge table and choose either result.
- **Level 18 — Spell Bombardment:** When a damage die rolls its maximum, roll an additional die and add it to spell damage.

## 🛠️ Metamagic options
- **Careful Spell:** Chosen creatures, up to your Charisma modifier, automatically succeed on a spell's saving throw. Useful for protecting allies in area spells.
- **Distant Spell:** Touch range becomes 30 feet; spells with a range can reach twice as far.
- **Empowered Spell:** Reroll up to your Charisma modifier in damage dice and accept the new results. It can be combined with another Metamagic.
- **Extended Spell:** Double duration up to 24 hours; helpful for buffs.
- **Heightened Spell:** Give a target disadvantage on a spell's saving throw, particularly useful for important spells like Hold Person.
- **Quickened Spell:** Change a one-action spell to a bonus action; the other spell that turn is restricted to a cantrip under the original rules.
- **Subtle Spell:** Cast without verbal or somatic components, useful where magic is restricted or stealth matters.
- **Twinned Spell:** Spend Sorcery Points to apply an eligible single-target spell to two creatures, for example Haste or Polymorph under the older rules.

## 🎯 Sorcerer playstyle
### 🚀 Burst damage
Fireball (3rd level) creates a 20-foot-radius blast. Lightning Bolt (3rd level) excels in narrow corridors. Meteor Swarm (9th level) offers tremendous burst damage. Clear areas quickly and reposition rather than prolonging combat.

### 🌀 Control and chaos
Hold Person (2nd level) paralyses targets; Hypnotic Pattern (3rd level) incapacitates opponents over a large area; Banishment (4th level) removes targets from combat, especially extraplanar creatures. Disable opponents at critical moments to make difficult encounters easier.

### ⚡ Mobility and speed
Low AC and hit points make mobility important. Misty Step (2nd level) teleports 30 feet as a bonus action; Fly (3rd level) lets you escape melee fighters; Teleport (7th level) quickly relocates the party. Stay ready to move.

### 🧙‍♂️ Customising magic
Metamagic distinguishes sorcerers from wizards and warlocks. Quickened Spell changes casting time, Twinned Spell applies eligible spells to two targets, such as Haste. Shape your spells for the situation: cast the right spell at the right moment.

### 🔥 Resilience and defence
Shield (1st level) provides an immediate +5 AC; Globe of Invulnerability (6th level) blocks lower-level spells; Counterspell (3rd level) interrupts another caster's spell. Respond quickly and decisively when you need protection.
