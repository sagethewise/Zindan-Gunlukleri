# 🎮 The Elder Scrolls IV: Oblivion Remastered
Bethesda's legendary RPG Oblivion returned in 2025 with modern graphics and renewed systems, offering nostalgia for veterans and discovery for newcomers.

## 📅 General information
- **Release:** 22 April 2025
- **Developers:** Bethesda Game Studios & Virtuos
- **Engine:** Unreal Engine 5
- **Platforms:** PC (Steam), Xbox Series X/S, PlayStation 5
- **Historical launch pricing:** Standard $49.99; Deluxe $59.99 including extra content and a digital artbook.
- **Game Pass:** Available on day one.

The original mentions a limited collector-oriented physical edition.

## ✨ Technical improvements
### Graphics and sound
- Forests, cities and dungeons remodelled with high-resolution textures and 4K support.
- Renewed dynamic lighting from sunlight to cave lanterns.
- More cinematic fog, rain and wind.
- Re-recorded NPC voices and environmental sounds such as birds, rivers and city ambience.

## 🎨 Visual comparison
| Feature | Original (2006) | Remastered (2025) |
|---|---|---|
| Engine | Gamebryo | Unreal Engine 5 |
| Resolution | 1024×768–1920×1080 | 1080p–4K (DLSS/FSR) |
| Textures | Low resolution (512×512) | Ultra HD (4K+) |
| Lighting | Fixed HDR effects | Real-time dynamic lighting |
| Shadows | Basic | High-quality SSAO, RT shadows |
| Environment | Sparse vegetation | Rich dynamic vegetation |
| Water/reflections | Static effects | Realistic physics and reflections |
| Particles | Basic smoke/fire | Detailed fog, sparks, spell particles |
| Animation | Basic skeletal | Smooth motion capture |
| Faces | Limited expressions | Dynamic expressions and eye movements |
| FPS | 30–60 | 60+ (144Hz monitor support) |
| HDR/shadow maps | None | HDR and global illumination |

The article praises the balance of optimisation and visual quality.

## 🧠 Gameplay improvements
- More rewarding, balanced leveling rather than simply spamming skills.
- NPC routines include work, conversation and shopping.
- Smoother sword, bow and spell combat; enemies use tactics, retreat and attack in teams.
- A streamlined HUD simplifies inventory, spells and maps.

## 🗺️ Content and expansions
- All DLCs included: Knights of the Nine (divine quests, mystical armour and holy battle) and Shivering Isles (Sheogorath's two-sided realm).
- The famous Horse Armor returns in updated form in the Deluxe edition.
- The original reports new side quests and a hidden dungeon based on developer notes.

## 🚀 Performance and hardware
- File size: approximately 119.2 GB with high-resolution packs.
- Steam Deck: described as fully supported with an optimised performance profile.
- Console SSDs offer nearly instantaneous transitions compared with the original.

## 🛍️ Purchase options at the time
- Steam digital edition.
- Fanatical preorder: 17% off, standard $41.49.
- Xbox / PlayStation stores: digital or physical editions.
- Deluxe contents: digital artbook, remastered soundtrack, Shivering Isles concept art and Imperial Horse Armor appearance.

Prices and availability above are historical, not current offers.

## 🧭 Conclusion
Oblivion Remastered demonstrates how a classic RPG can be modernised. Though not revolutionary in every aspect, fidelity to the series, smooth operation on modern devices and enriched atmosphere make it appealing to both veteran and new fans.

> It could be the best Elder Scrolls experience in the years before TES VI.
