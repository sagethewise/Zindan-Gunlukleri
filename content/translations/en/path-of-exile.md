# 🧭 Path of Exile beginner's guide

## ⚔️ What is Path of Exile?
Path of Exile (PoE) is a free action RPG with Diablo-like mechanics and an enormous skill tree.

### 💡 Starting tips
- Choose your class carefully.
- The economy is detailed; use trade websites.
- Each league starts with fresh characters.

### 🧠 Why play?
Deep mechanics, regularly updated content, and rich itemisation and crafting systems.

*The first instalment of a detailed beginner's guide.*

## 🎮 1. Overview
Developed and published by Grinding Gear Games, Path of Exile is a free-to-play action RPG inspired by Diablo II. It features real-time action, complex character progression, an enormous passive skill tree, modular skills and regularly updated leagues.

### What is a league?
Leagues generally start every three months, introduce new mechanics and rewards, and have players begin with new characters.

## ⚔️ 2. Basic gameplay
- Isometric camera; mouse and keyboard controls.
- Defeat enemies, collect items and develop your character.
- Rarities: Normal (white), Magic (blue), Rare (yellow), Unique (orange).
- Use a customised loot filter through [FilterBlade](https://filterblade.xyz).

## 🔍 3. Character classes

| Class | Main stat | Playstyle |
|---|---|---|
| Marauder | Strength | Tank, melee |
| Ranger | Dexterity | Fast, ranged |
| Witch | Intelligence | Magic, minions |
| Duelist | Str + Dex | Fast melee |
| Templar | Str + Int | Hybrid magic and physical |
| Shadow | Dex + Int | Traps, poison, critical hits |
| Scion | All | Advanced class |

Recommended starters: Marauder–Juggernaut, Witch–Necromancer, Ranger–Deadeye.

## 🌳 4. Passive skill tree
- One tree contains over 1,200 nodes.
- Classes begin at different points.
- The tree is divided into STR, DEX and INT regions.
- Cluster Jewels extend the outer ring.

## 💎 5. Skill gems and sockets
Active skills are gems inserted into equipment. Skill Gems grant active abilities; Support Gems enhance them.

Socket colours: red = STR, green = DEX, blue = INT. Linked sockets matter; support gems strengthen compatible skills:
- Fireball (blue) + Spell Echo Support (blue)
- Cleave (red) + Melee Physical Damage Support (red)

## ⚙️ 6. Items and trading
- Rarities: Normal → Magic → Rare → Unique.
- Items may have affixes such as “+1 to socketed gems.”
- Quality is expressed as a percentage for armour, evasion and gems.
- Five flask slots offer health, mana, speed and defensive flasks; they can be reused after refilling.
- Trading historically uses currency items rather than gold. Chaos Orb is a common currency. [Official trade site](https://www.pathofexile.com/trade).

## 🧱 7. Leagues
Leagues are seasonal game modes, generally lasting three months and introducing a new mechanic.
- **Challenge League:** Popular fresh-start seasonal mode.
- **Standard:** Permanent mode receiving characters after leagues end.
- **Hardcore:** Death sends characters to Standard.
- **Solo Self-Found (SSF):** No trading or parties.

Beginners are advised to try a softcore challenge trade league.

## 🌍 8. Map system (Atlas)
The endgame revolves around maps opened with the Map Device.
- Tiers 1–16.
- Bosses: Shaper, Elder, Sirus, Maven.
- Customise the experience via the Atlas Passive Tree.

## 🛡️ 9. Survival strategies
Dying is easy; survival takes care. Aim for 75% elemental resistance, build Chaos Resistance for the endgame, keep track of flasks and use movement skills such as Flame Dash and Dash.
