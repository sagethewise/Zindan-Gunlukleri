# 🎮 Build introduction
Spiritborn offers high mobility and strong ranged attacks. Quill Volley maximises area damage, while critical chance, attack speed and Paragon synergy support solo and group play. This guide aims to prepare players for Nightmare Dungeons, Helltide and boss farming.

## 📋 Build summary
| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| Build type | Ranged, critical-hit focused |
| Main skill | Quill Volley |
| Playstyle | Speed, accurate AoE, kiting, critical hits |
| Paragon theme | Critical hits, spirit tree, mobility |
| Suitable content | Nightmare Dungeons, Helltide, World Bosses |

## 🎯 Who is it for?
Players looking for high damage and mobility, critical-hit play, ranged clearing and area control, and quick World Boss kills.

## 🧬 Skills (1–60)
**Quill Volley:** High area damage; requires careful resource management. Upgrade it as soon as it becomes available. The original suggests Razorfeather Barrage for clearing speed.

## 🧠 Skill tree
### Tier 1: Starting skills
**Spirit Dart:** Generates energy and marks enemies. Suggested passive: Piercing Darts, for multi-target hits.

### Tier 2: Quill Volley
**Quill Volley:** The core of the build; hits multiple targets. Suggested passive: Volley of Thorns, adding critical chance and piercing.

### Tier 3: Support and buffs
- **Spirit Surge:** Increases attack speed and critical damage; Surging Spirits improves energy regeneration.
- **Windstep:** Helps with dodging, positioning and boss mechanics; Gale Step adds damage after a dodge.

### Tier 4: Critical support passives
- **Hunter's Precision:** The original describes up to 30% increased critical damage.
- **Spirit Overflow:** Increases energy capacity for longer sustained attacks.

## 🧭 Gear recommendations
Prioritise critical chance and spirit regeneration, movement-speed boots, a rare bow early on and legendary aspects in midgame. The original suggests Aspect of Twin Volley or Aspect of Razor Plume, claiming a 40% improvement.

### Suggested skill route
Spirit Dart → Quill Volley → Windstep → Spirit Surge → Hunter's Precision → Spirit Overflow.
